## Why you'll want it

Developers don't live in one terminal anymore. You juggle a build here, logs there, a chat with
your agent in the corner — and spend half your day herding windows. Vivida gives you one calm home
for all of it:

- **Workspaces that think like you do.** Group projects the way you think about them — one
  workspace per project, with tabs and splits inside. Name them, rename them, and find anything at
  a glance in the sidebar.
- **A terminal that shows, not just tells.** Vivida renders images, video, and audio right in your
  panes. Preview a chart, watch a render, or listen to a clip without leaving your keyboard.
- **Fast and easy on the eyes.** GPU-rendered and polished, so a wall of output stays smooth and
  readable all day long.
- **Zero setup for your agents.** The automation tools are built in. Nothing to install, nothing
  to configure — your agent is already at home.

## A terminal built for AI agents

Most terminals leave agents blind: they can spew text, but they can't see the results, find the
right pane, or clean up after themselves. Vivida changes that. An agent running in one pane can:

- **See** — take screenshots of any pane and actually check its own work
- **Act** — type, press keys, and move the mouse anywhere in the workspace
- **Navigate** — find panes by workspace, tab, or position in deeply nested splits
- **Organize** — open, arrange, and manage tabs and splits as it works
- **Follow through** — execute whole multi-step plans and verify each one

The result: agents that finish the job instead of guessing. They confirm the build went green,
spot the error in the log, open the right pane, and fix it — while you watch or walk away.

## Better together

You get a workspace that feels great to drive. Your agents get eyes and hands. Vivida is where
both of you get more done.

## Keyboard shortcuts

`Mod` is `Cmd` on macOS and `Ctrl` elsewhere. The gear in the tab bar opens the same list in a
scrollable window, alongside the Vivido terminal bindings that reach a pane.

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Mod T` | New tab |
| `Mod D` | Split the pane left and right |
| `Mod Shift D` | Split the pane top and bottom |
| `Mod W` | Close the focused pane |
| `Mod Shift N` | New workspace |
| `Mod Shift W` | Close the workspace |
| `Mod Shift B` | Expand, shrink, or hide the sidebar |
| `Mod Shift ]` / `Mod Shift [` | Next or previous tab |
| `Mod 1` – `Mod 9` | Switch to workspace 1 through 9 |
| `Ctrl Shift F12` | Recover a stuck terminal |
